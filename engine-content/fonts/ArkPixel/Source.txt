Ark Pixel Font 2026.10.25 by TakWolf (SIL Open Font License 1.1, see OFL.txt).
https://github.com/TakWolf/ark-pixel-font

The four fonts are the 10 px and 12 px sizes in Monospaced and Proportional
widths, Latin language flavor (Chinese, Japanese and Korean characters use the
Latin flavor's shapes), with every character the font draws:

  ark-pixel-10px-monospaced-latin.woff2
  ark-pixel-10px-proportional-latin.woff2
  ark-pixel-12px-monospaced-latin.woff2
  ark-pixel-12px-proportional-latin.woff2

They were built unmodified from the human-drawn glyph sources at commit
f47afb1241859b5311dc48e9571e5fbaf8f82b32 with the project's own build program
(MIT License): `python -m tools.build_fonts --font-formats otf.woff2` on
Python 3.14, renaming `<name>.otf.woff2` to `<name>.woff2`. OFL.txt is the
repository's LICENSE-OFL.

Projects and exports that ship these fonts must keep OFL.txt with them.
