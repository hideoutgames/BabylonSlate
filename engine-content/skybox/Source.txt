Kenney Skyboxes "skybox-day" (CC0, see License.txt), the engine default sky.

px.png ... nz.png = the 4096 x 2048 equirectangular panorama reprojected into
six 1024 x 1024 cube faces in Babylon CubeTexture order, bilinear sampled at
2 x 2 per pixel. The panorama is turned about +Y so its sun disc lies toward
DEFAULT_SCENE_SUN_POSITION (packages/core/src/skybox.ts); move both together.

cubemap_layout.png is not part of the pack: it is the Skybox Creator mapping
diagram.

pack's own (line endings normalised to LF).
